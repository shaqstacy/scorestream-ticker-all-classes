From the Kentucky Mesa back home to the Ohio River

The Stallings and Zoglman families: English Catholics who reached Maryland, German farmers from Bavaria, and the Kentucky brothers who built a Catholic colony in a dugout near the Navajo Nation. Their lines meet in Theresa Lavina Stallings and Edward “Bud” Zoglman, married in 1938, and continue through their daughter Libby into the Folz family of St. Louis and Evansville.
