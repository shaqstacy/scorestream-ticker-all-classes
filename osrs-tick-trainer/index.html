What you're drilling

  1. Your weapon timer keeps running while you move. After an attack, a 4-tick bow has 3 free ticks. Step out of a shadow on one of those, then click the Leviathan again, and the next shot still fires on time.
  2. Never move on your attack tick. A move click that lands on the tick your weapon is ready replaces the attack, and the strip turns red.
  3. The last click in a tick wins. Clicking a tile and then the boss inside the same 0.6 s means you only attack. Plan the move one tick earlier.
  4. You run 2 tiles per tick. A 3×3 drop centred on you needs a 2-tile run or a diagonal step. Running back into range costs a tick before you can fire.
  5. Phases speed up. Debris falls every 10, 8, then 6 ticks as the Leviathan drops below 66% and 33%. Each threshold stuns it for 8 ticks with no debris and +25% damage, so stand still and fire.

Movement is simplified to straight-line steps, and prayers are left out for now. Boss volleys are shown so you can see the rhythm, but they don't deal damage yet. Controls: click or tap a tile to move, click the Leviathan to attack, Space pauses, R restarts.
