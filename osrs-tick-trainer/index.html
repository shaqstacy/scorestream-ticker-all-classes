How the fight runs

  1. Volleys. The Leviathan fires a volley of orbs from its pool while rocks fall on you. Rock shadows count down in ticks; step off before they reach 0.
  2. Stun before the volley ends. Select Shadow Barrage (F) and click the boss. If the volley finishes first, boulders drop in a plus shape centred on you.
  3. Hit the wound. A stun lasts 15 ticks. Run around the pool to its back (the north side) and attack the glowing wound for boosted damage.
  4. Specials alternate after each stun. Lightning chases you around the pool one tile per tick. You run two, so each tick you stand still to attack costs one tile of lead. In the smoke blast, debris lands around you and stays; stand so a rock sits between you and the boss before the blast.
  5. Enrage at 180 HP. Orbs come every 2 ticks and the abyssal pathfinder appears. Stay inside its purple area: outside it, orbs hit you. Enraged lightning moves 3 tiles every 2 ticks, so you get fewer free ticks.
  6. Tick rules. Your weapon timer keeps counting while you move. A move click on your attack tick replaces the attack. The last click in a tick wins.

Prayers aren't in yet, so orbs assume perfect prayer outside of enrage. Controls: click or tap a tile to move, click the Leviathan to attack, F selects the shadow spell, Space pauses, R restarts.
